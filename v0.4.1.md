# v0.4.1

<!-- shuku:locale=zh-CN:start -->
## 简体中文

本版本改进书库整理体验，新增智能书架创建、可复用筛选规则、实时匹配预览和更灵活的响应式书架布局。

TXT 导入现在能更可靠地处理 GBK、GB18030、多字节采样边界、文件尾部 NUL 填充，以及仍在写入中的文件。

同时优化了延迟加载的导入偏好、移动端与 PWA 导航，并补齐简体中文和英文文案。
<!-- shuku:locale=zh-CN:end -->

<!-- shuku:locale=en-US:start -->
## English

This release improves library organization with smart-shelf creation, reusable filter rules, live match previews, and more responsive shelf layouts.

TXT importing is now more resilient for GBK and GB18030 books, multibyte sample boundaries, trailing NUL padding, and files that are still being written.

It also refines lazy-loaded import preferences, mobile and PWA navigation coverage, and complete zh-CN and en-US copy.
