# v1.6.1

<!-- shuku:locale=zh-CN:start -->
## 修复：

1. 修复 iPhone 主屏幕 PWA 切换到滚动阅读后，正文无法上下滑动、出现大片空白并在松手后弹回的问题。
2. 修复手机网页阅读器点击正文左右翻页区域跨章时，一次点击翻两页、正文闪烁和跳页的问题。
3. 修复滚动模式跨章后继续点击翻页时，可能提前跳出当前长章节的问题。
4. 统一分页与滚动模式的正文左右边距，修复切换阅读方式后边距不一致的问题。
5. 修复 iPhone 主屏幕 PWA 阅读器底部留白过大及底部控制栏显示不完整的问题。

## Fixed:

1. Fixed vertical scrolling in the iPhone Home Screen PWA after switching to scrolling mode. The reader no longer shows a large blank area and snaps back when the finger is released.
2. Fixed single taps on the left or right page-turn zones advancing twice across chapter boundaries, causing flicker and skipped pages in the mobile web reader.
3. Fixed page-turn taps in scrolling mode skipping out of a long chapter after crossing a chapter boundary.
4. Made body margins consistent between paginated and scrolling modes.
5. Fixed excessive bottom whitespace and partially obscured bottom controls in the iPhone Home Screen PWA reader.
