# v1.5.2

<!-- shuku:locale=zh-CN:start -->
## 新增：

1. 在「关于」页面提交功能建议或报告问题，支持图片、附件、可编辑说明和提交前核对。
2. 管理员可从系统错误日志发起反馈，核对关联事件和图书信息后提交；也可以仅手动描述问题。
3. 可选填写 QQ 号、交流群昵称或邮箱，并自行决定是否附带系统环境；安装方式可选择应用商店、手动安装或 Docker。

## 修复：

1. 「关于」页面将反馈入口放在更新与版本之前，版本列表仅显示最近五项。

## Added:

1. Submit suggestions or report problems from About, with images, attachments, editable descriptions, and a review step before sending.
2. Administrators can report an error from system logs after reviewing related events and books, or describe a problem manually.
3. Optionally provide a QQ number, group nickname, or email, and choose whether to include system details. Installation method can be selected as app store, manual, or Docker.

## Fixed:

1. The feedback entry now appears above Updates and Versions in About, and the version list shows only the latest five entries.
