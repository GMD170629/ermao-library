# v1.6.0

<!-- shuku:locale=zh-CN:start -->
## 新增：

1. 书库删除改为后台清理，提交后立即从列表中移除，清理可在服务重启后继续，原始图书文件保持不变。

## 修复：

1. 提升 TXT 导入和阅读兼容性，支持 GBK／GB18030、UTF-8 及带 BOM 的 UTF-16；遇到坏字节显示为 `�` 并继续读取，不再因坏字节比例、NUL 或乱码特征拒绝文件，保留正常文字和原始文件。
2. 修正 TXT 编码探测日志，正常的 UTF-8 探测失败和容错替换不再产生错误日志。
3. 减少无用元数据查询存档和历史日志数据的存储占用，并将过期元数据、预览缓存的后台清理调整为每日执行，减少重复扫描。

<!-- shuku:locale=en-US:start -->
## Added:

1. Library deletion now cleans up in the background. Deleted libraries disappear from lists immediately, cleanup resumes after a service restart, and original book files remain unchanged.

## Fixed:

1. Improved TXT import and reading compatibility for GBK/GB18030, UTF-8, and UTF-16 with a BOM. Invalid bytes appear as `�` while reading continues. Files are no longer rejected based on replacement rates, NUL characters, or garbled-text heuristics, preserving readable text and original files.
2. Corrected TXT encoding detection logs so expected UTF-8 probe failures and tolerant replacements no longer produce error logs.
3. Reduced storage used by obsolete metadata lookup archives and historical logs. Expired metadata and preview caches are now cleaned up daily to avoid repeated scans.
