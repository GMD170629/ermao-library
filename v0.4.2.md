# v0.4.2

<!-- shuku:locale=zh-CN:start -->
## 简体中文

本版本扩展了书库整理能力，新增嵌套书架集合、更完整的书架成员操作，以及基于稳定分组查询的系列与作者浏览入口。

导入队列现在支持协调式清空：系统会暂停新任务，并等待正在运行的任务安全完成或回滚，同时改进工作进程的健康状态与持久化可见性。

生产交付补齐独立镜像优化、响应式与 PWA 覆盖、完整中英文文案，并移除已经废弃的设计和原型资源。

## English

This release expands library organization with nested shelf collections, richer shelf membership actions, and dedicated series and author browsing backed by stable grouping queries.

Import operations now include a coordinated queue-clear workflow that pauses intake and waits for active work to finish or roll back safely, with improved worker health visibility and persistence.

Production delivery is more reliable with standalone image optimization support, refreshed responsive and PWA coverage, complete zh-CN and en-US copy, and cleanup of obsolete design and prototype artifacts.
